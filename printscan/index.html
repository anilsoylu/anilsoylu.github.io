PrintScan

Print a booklet, a poster, or four pages on one sheet, straight from your iPhone.

PrintScan lays the pages out the way the paper needs them: fold-ready booklets, posters split across several sheets, 2, 4 or 6 pages per sheet, manual duplex in the right order, and ID cards at real size.

Scan with the camera or import a PDF, reorder and rotate the pages, then print on the printer you already use. Documents stay on the device, and text recognition runs on your iPhone.

Free, with no limit and no watermark

  • Scan and import as many documents as you want
  • Reorder, rotate, delete, merge and split pages
  • Print a single page
  • Folders, tags and search
  • Face ID app lock

The subscription unlocks four jobs

  • Print layout: booklet, poster, 2, 4 or 6 per sheet, duplex order, photo sheets, ID card
  • Sign and fill: draw your signature once, then place it, your initials, the date or text on any page
  • Searchable PDF: text recognition on the device adds an invisible text layer
  • Password on export: send a PDF that asks for a password before it opens

Price

$29.99 per year or $4.99 per month, both giving the same access. Payment is charged to your Apple Account. It renews unless you turn auto-renew off at least 24 hours before the period ends, and you manage it in your Apple Account settings. There is no free trial.

Requirements

iPhone running iOS 16 or later. Printing goes through AirPrint, to the printer already set up on your device.
