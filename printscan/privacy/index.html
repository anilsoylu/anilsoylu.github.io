PrintScan Privacy Policy

Effective 15 September 2026.

PrintScan is an iPhone app published by Anıl Soylu. This policy covers the app and its share extension. There is no PrintScan account, no sign-in, and no PrintScan server.

Your documents stay on the device

Every scan is captured, corrected, stored, recognised and printed on your iPhone. Text recognition uses Apple's Vision framework, which runs on the device. Documents, page images, recognised text, document titles, folder names and signatures are never uploaded.

Scans are written to the app's own folder inside your iPhone's Documents directory, so they are included in your iCloud or encrypted local backups, under Apple's terms. PrintScan does not sync them anywhere else. Deleting a document from the app, and then emptying the trash, deletes its files from the device.

When you print, the pages are sent to the printer you select over AirPrint on your own network. When you share or export, the file goes to whatever destination you pick in the iOS share sheet; from that point it is covered by that app or service's own policy.

Permissions the app asks for

Permission Why
Camera To scan a document. The camera is used only while the scanner is open, and the captured pages are stored on the device.
Add to Photos Only if you choose to save scanned pages as images. PrintScan never reads your photo library; pages you import through the photo picker are handed to the app by iOS, one selection at a time.
Face ID or Touch ID Only if you turn on the app lock. iOS performs the match and tells the app whether it succeeded. Your biometric data never reaches the app.

What does leave the device

PrintScan itself makes no network requests. Three third-party SDKs do, and this is what they send. None of it is linked to your identity and none of it is used to track you across apps or websites.

Sentry — crash and error reports

When the app crashes or hits an error, Sentry receives the stack trace, the app version, the iOS version and the device model. Screenshots, view-hierarchy capture and personally identifying information are all turned off in the app's configuration, so no part of a scanned page can be attached to a report. Document titles and folder names are stripped from the diagnostic breadcrumbs before a report is sent. Sentry privacy policy.

PostHog — anonymous product analytics

PostHog receives a small fixed set of events so I can see which features are used: a scan finishing, a signature being applied, a print starting, the paywall appearing, a purchase completing and a restore completing. Each event carries counts and fixed values such as the page count, the chosen layout, and where the action started from. No document title, file name, recognised text or anything you typed or drew is ever included.

Events go to PostHog's EU region. Every event is sent with IP-based location lookup disabled, so no location is derived from it. No profile is created for you: the app never identifies a user and screen-view autocapture is off. PostHog privacy policy.

RevenueCat — subscriptions

If you subscribe, Apple handles the payment; PrintScan never sees your card, your Apple Account or your billing address. RevenueCat receives the purchase and renewal state of the subscription so the app can answer whether this install is entitled to the paid features. The app never sets a user ID, so RevenueCat holds only an anonymous per-install identifier. RevenueCat privacy policy.

Data collection summary

This is the same disclosure shown on the App Store listing. Nothing is linked to your identity and nothing is used for tracking.

Data Purpose
Purchase history App functionality (RevenueCat)
Product interaction and other usage data Analytics (PostHog)
Crash, performance and other diagnostic data App functionality (Sentry)

Contact information, location, contacts, browsing history, identifiers and user content — which includes your documents and scans — are not collected.

Retention

Crash reports, analytics events and subscription records are retained by Sentry, PostHog and RevenueCat under their own retention schedules, linked above. Because none of that data is linked to you, it cannot be traced back to an individual on request. Everything else lives only on your iPhone, for as long as you keep it there.

Children

PrintScan is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version is published on this page with a new effective date, before the app version it describes is released.
