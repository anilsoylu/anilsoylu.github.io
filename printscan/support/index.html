Email business.anilsoylu@gmail.com. Include your iPhone model, your iOS version and the PrintScan version from Settings.

Subscriptions

Manage or cancel

The subscription is billed by Apple, so it is managed in your Apple Account, not in the app. Open Apple Account subscriptions, or go to Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Turn auto-renew off at least 24 hours before the period ends.

I already paid but the features are locked

Open Settings inside PrintScan and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with. If it still does not unlock, email me with the date of purchase.

Refunds

Refunds are issued by Apple, not by me. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Printing

My printer does not appear

PrintScan prints through AirPrint and offers whatever printers iOS finds. The iPhone and the printer have to be on the same Wi-Fi network, and the printer has to support AirPrint. If the printer is missing from the iOS print dialog in any other app as well, it is not a PrintScan problem.

The paper size is wrong

Choose the paper size in PrintScan's print settings before printing. PrintScan forces that size through to the printer, so a printer that defaults to Letter still prints the A4 you asked for, provided the paper is loaded.

The booklet pages come out in the wrong order

A booklet has to be printed double-sided. If your printer has no duplex unit, use manual duplex: PrintScan orders the sheets so you print one side, flip the stack, and print the other.

Documents

Where are my scans stored

On the iPhone, inside the app's own storage. They are included in your device backup. Nothing is uploaded — see the privacy policy.

I deleted a document by mistake

Deleted documents go to the trash in the library and can be restored from there until you empty it. Emptying the trash is permanent.

The app lock will not let me in

The lock uses Face ID or Touch ID with your device passcode as the fallback, both handled by iOS. If biometrics fail, use the passcode. The app has no separate password to recover.

Language

PrintScan follows your iPhone language and can also be set to a different one from inside its Settings screen.

Bugs and feature requests

Both go to business.anilsoylu@gmail.com. For a bug, describe what you did and what happened instead; a screen recording helps.
